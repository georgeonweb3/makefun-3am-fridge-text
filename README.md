One line, 140 characters max: the message a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am. The entry program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line: *"3am. You opened me at 2:47, stared for 41 seconds, and closed me without taking anything. I have logged this as a cry for help."*

Why this line: it was picked by a blind pairwise tournament of six candidates judged only against the judge brief (5/5 wins). It has two concrete anchors (2:47, 41 seconds), three beats (opened, stared, logged), and ends on the punch — the fridge, passive and shameless at 3am, filing its owner under "cry for help". It avoids the obvious angles about milk, ice, or a door left open, and it stands alone as one lock-screen line with no follow-up needed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
